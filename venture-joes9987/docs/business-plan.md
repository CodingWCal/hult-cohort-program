# Business plan — EudaLearn

**Company / product:** EudaLearn (venture of @joes9987)  
**Stage:** Pre-seed / pilot · Hult Summer Pilot 2026  
**Live app:** https://learn-joes9987.vercel.app  
**Listing:** https://www.ludwitt.com/dashboard/marketplace/eudalearn

## 1. Executive summary

EudaLearn sells *deliberate practice* for AI-era builders: short modules on prompt briefs, launch trust, event telemetry, and suite thinking. Identity and AI coach tips run through **Ludwitt** (BYOB). Engineers earn **70%** of credit spend; Ludwitt takes 30%. The Euda suite (Market, PM, Chat) is distribution and proof we can ship. Near-term goal: ≥25 qualified external practicers and first investor conversations; 12-month goal: cohort B2B packs + weekly retained practice.

## 2. Problem & solution

**Problem:** Builders use AI tools daily but do not practice the operational skills that make those tools reliable. Long courses are too slow; chat tips are unmeasured.

**Solution:** 7–10 minute modules with quiz + optional Ludwitt coach tip; sessions instrumented for platform metrics; one-click Ludwitt sign-in via approved marketplace listing.

## 3. Market & competition

See [market-research.md](market-research.md). Wedge is Ludwitt-native practice, not generic LMS. Competitors are long courses, Notion playbooks, and unstructured ChatGPT use.

## 4. Business model & pricing

| Motion | How money moves |
|--------|-----------------|
| **BYOB (now)** | Learner spends Ludwitt paid credits on AI coach; app receives ~70% of charge |
| **Free practice core** | Lesson + quiz free to maximize qualified sessions and distribution |
| **B2B packs (Y1)** | Cohort operators buy credit bundles + private module sets |
| **Suite attach** | EudaMarket drives discovery; Learn captures practice |

Pricing experiments: coach tip after quiz (~fraction of a cent to a few cents in credits depending on tokens). Core modules remain free to clear adoption gates.

## 5. Go-to-market

1. Ludwitt marketplace listing (approved)  
2. EudaMarket `/suite` CTAs  
3. Founder-led LinkedIn / community asks (non-cohort)  
4. Pilot with other Hult-like programs after ≥25–100 WAUs  
5. Content loops: new module every 1–2 weeks

## 6. Unit economics (labeled assumptions)

| Metric | Assumption | Note |
|--------|------------|------|
| CAC (organic pilot) | $0–5 | Sweat equity + suite cross-link |
| CAC (paid later) | $15–40 | Only after retention proven |
| Credit spend / active user / mo | $1–4 | Coach tips + future features |
| Take rate to EudaLearn | 70% of charged credits | Ludwitt BYOB |
| Gross margin on credit rev | ~85%+ | Infra is Next/Vercel/Supabase |
| LTV (Y1 organic) | $10–30 | Low until B2B packs |

These are planning numbers for conversation, not audited financials.

## 10. Risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|------------|
| Platform dependency (Ludwitt) | BYOB contract clarity; app-owned events store; suite still distributes |
| OAuth / marketplace regressions | Health checks; documented fallbacks for graders |
| Low retention after 1 module | Roadmap Q1 habit loops |
| Anti-gaming on user counts | Exclude self/cohort; campaign tags; honest snapshots |
| Credit spend too low | Free core + paid coach; B2B packs |
