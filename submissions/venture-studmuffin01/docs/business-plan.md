# Business plan: AI Prompting Academy

## Executive summary

**AI Prompting Academy** is an MVP workplace AI LMS that helps professionals master prompt craft for Microsoft Copilot and other assistants. The first course teaches the **SCORE** method (*Prompt Like a Pro*). We sell **individual access** and **team seats** to managers and L&D buyers who need reliable AI output without multi-week training programs.

## Problem & solution

- **Problem:** Vague prompts → weak AI drafts, invented details, non-forwardable updates.
- **Solution:** A short, structured LMS path (SCORE) plus personal SCORE habits, sold as Academy access for individuals and teams.

## Market & competition

See [`market-research.md`](market-research.md). Positioning: shorter and more workplace-specific than MOOCs; more structured than free prompt tips; productized vs internal slide decks.

## Go-to-market

1. Ship production Academy (extend Week 4 app) with `/pricing`.
2. Direct outreach to managers in ops, consulting, and client-facing roles.
3. LinkedIn + professional networks; Hult alumni wedge.
4. Capture ≥25 qualified external learners on venture metrics.
5. Convert early users into interview quotes and design partners.

## Unit economics (assumptions — label clearly)

- **CAC (hypothesis):** $15–40 via organic/LinkedIn founder-led sales in pilot.
- **LTV (hypothesis):** Individual $29–108/yr; Team $199–799/yr depending on renewal.
- **Gross margin:** Software LMS ≈ high (hosting + support); AI API costs low while curriculum is mostly static SCORE content.
- **Payback:** Target &lt; 2 months on team deals in pilot.
