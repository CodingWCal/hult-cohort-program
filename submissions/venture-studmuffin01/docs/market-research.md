# Market research: AI Prompting Academy

## Problem

Professionals are expected to use Microsoft Copilot and other AI assistants at work, but vague prompts produce unreliable drafts, invented facts, and outputs that managers cannot forward. Generic ChatGPT tips and long AI courses do not teach a short, repeatable workplace method. Teams waste time rewriting AI output instead of shipping decisions.

## ICP

- **Primary:** Managers, supervisors, analysts, and client-facing professionals who already have Copilot/ChatGPT access and must produce emails, status updates, and briefs.
- **Buyer (B2B):** L&D / Ops / department leads buying team seats.
- **Buyer (B2C):** Individual professionals paying for self-paced access.
- **Geo (SOM start):** English-speaking remote-friendly; Hult/Boston–London network as wedge.

## Wedge

Ship a **60-minute SCORE curriculum** already proven in a cohort build, packaged as **AI Prompting Academy** with individual + team pricing — faster time-to-competence than a multi-week MOOC, more structured than prompt tips.
