## AI usage frequency (n=35)

Respondents skewed toward regular workplace use (**Weekly** most common), with Daily / Rarely / Multiple× per day also represented.

## Biggest challenges (select up to 2; themes by frequency)

Recurring barriers (ordered by how often they appeared in the export):

1. **Responses are too generic**
2. **I don’t fully trust the results**
3. **I spend too much time rewriting outputs**
4. **I don’t know how to structure prompts effectively**
5. **Responses are inaccurate** / **AI doesn’t understand my context**

**Insight:** Access to AI tools is not the bottleneck. Prompt effectiveness, trust, and rewrite burden are.

## Time spent editing / correcting AI output each week

Spread across:

- Less than 30 minutes  
- 30 minutes to 1 hour  
- 1 to 3 hours  
- 3 to 5 hours (smaller set)

A meaningful minority reports **multi-hour** weekly rewrite cost.

## Willingness to pay (stated “reasonable price” for practical training)

Answers clustered mainly at:

- **Free only**
- **Under TT$150**
- **TT$150 – TT$300** (meaningful minority)
- At least one **TT$300 – TT$700**

**Interpretation:** There is price sensitivity; free and sub‑TT$150 dominate. A minority accepts **TT$150–300**, which is in the same ballpark as a low-ticket individual pilot (Academy hypothesis ~USD $29). Do **not** claim broad demand at TT$700+.

## Pilot session intent

Separate question on joining a live pilot in the next few weeks:

- Majority: **free session only** or not interested  
- Minority: would **pay under TT$150** or **TT$150–300**  
- At least one: would **pay TT$300+**
