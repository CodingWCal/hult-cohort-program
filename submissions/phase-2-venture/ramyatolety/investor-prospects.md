7. **Omidyar Network India** — Equity + grant investor explicitly funding financial-inclusion products for small/marginal farmers and the "aspirational Indian." Strong strategic (not just financial) fit. Contact: omidyarnetwork.in (contact/portfolio page).
8. **Flourish Ventures** — $850M AUM global fintech fund with an explicit financial-health/inclusion mandate and active India focus (spun out of Omidyar Network's fintech investing). Contact: flourishventures.com.
9. **Caspian Impact Investments** — India impact-debt/equity investor with an agri and agri-value-chain livelihoods focus; relevant for the harvest-to-stipend and insurance lines. Contact: caspian.in.
10. **Catalyst Fund** (backed by BFA Global, with Accion, Gates Foundation, Omidyar Network, JPMorgan, Quona, Grey Ghost Ventures as collaborators) — grants + advisory (up to ~$100K) specifically for pre-investable inclusive fintech startups; a plausible pre-seed/pre-VC step.
