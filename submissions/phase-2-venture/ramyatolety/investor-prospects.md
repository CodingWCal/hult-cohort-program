## Specialist financial-inclusion and fintech-for-inclusion investors

1. **Accion Venture Lab**: a seed-stage global fund (Accion) dedicated entirely to financial-inclusion fintech, active in South Asia including India, with typical checks of about $300K to $500K. Strong thematic fit for the four-product bundle. Contact via accion.org, the Venture Lab program page or team contact.
2. **Quona Capital**: a fintech-for-financial-inclusion VC with 70 or more portfolio companies across Latin America, India, and Southeast Asia and Africa. Co-founder Ganesh Rengaswamy previously worked at Lok Capital and Unitus. Directly relevant thesis and India experience. Contact via quona.com's contact page.
3. **Elevar Equity**: an impact-focused VC investing in financial inclusion and essential services in India and Latin America since the early days of MFI investing. Contact via elevarequity.com.
4. **Unitus Ventures** (formerly Unitus Seed Fund): an India-based seed fund with an "Include India" investment vertical explicitly targeting startups serving underserved populations, including rural and informal-economy fintech. Contact via unitusventures.com.
5. **Aavishkaar Capital**: one of India's oldest impact VCs, with a thesis spanning financial inclusion, agriculture, and essential services across India and emerging Asia and Africa, and a long track record with MFI-adjacent fintech. Contact via aavishkaarcapital.com.
6. **Lok Capital**: an India-focused venture and growth fund investing in financial inclusion and social enterprises since the mid-2000s, with a deep MFI and NBFC network. Contact via lokcapital.com.
7. **Omidyar Network India**: an equity and grant investor explicitly funding financial-inclusion products for small and marginal farmers and the "aspirational Indian." Strong strategic, not just financial, fit. Contact via omidyarnetwork.in's contact or portfolio page.
8. **Flourish Ventures**: an $850M AUM global fintech fund with an explicit financial-health and inclusion mandate and an active India focus (spun out of Omidyar Network's fintech investing). Contact via flourishventures.com.
9. **Caspian Impact Investments**: an India impact debt and equity investor with an agri and agri-value-chain livelihoods focus, relevant for the harvest-to-stipend and insurance lines. Contact via caspian.in.
10. **Catalyst Fund** (backed by BFA Global, with Accion, the Gates Foundation, Omidyar Network, JPMorgan, Quona, and Grey Ghost Ventures as collaborators): grants and advisory support, up to about $100K, specifically for pre-investable inclusive fintech startups. A plausible pre-seed and pre-VC step. Contact via the BFA Global or Accion program pages.

## Agri-fintech and rural-focused investors

11. **Omnivore**: India's longest-running agrifoodtech VC, with a thesis that explicitly includes rural fintech and climate-smart agriculture, and seed and Series A checks of $1M to $5M (likely above this venture's current stage, but relevant for a later round or an advisory intro). Contact via omnivore.vc.
12. **Ankur Capital**: a Mumbai-based pre-seed and seed fund investing in "transformative technologies for the next billion Indians" across agritech, fintech, and digital inclusion, with check sizes of $200K to $1M that fit this stage well. Contact via ankurcapital.com.
13. **Samunnati** (a strategic and corp-dev angle, not a fund): an agri value-chain financing platform already in the competitive landscape, worth a strategic-partnership conversation rather than a pure fundraising pitch, given adjacency rather than direct competition on the household-lending side. Contact via samunnati.com.

## Generalist India VCs with an active fintech-inclusion thesis

14. **Kalaari Capital**: a Bangalore VC explicitly citing inclusion and accessibility as part of its fintech thesis, with a record of backing digital lending and payments startups serving underserved populations. Contact via kalaari.com.
15. **Blume Ventures**: an early-stage India generalist VC with fintech investments spanning consumer lending and inclusion-adjacent products, useful for a general pre-seed or seed conversation. Contact via blume.vc.
16. **Better Capital** (Vaibhav Domkundwar): an India-focused early-stage and pre-seed generalist known for fast decisions and small first checks. Worth approaching for a pre-seed round given speed and stage fit, though not an inclusion specialist. Contact via bettercapital.vc.
17. **3one4 Capital**: a Bangalore early-stage VC with fintech portfolio exposure, a general pre-seed and seed fit for India-based founders. Contact via 3one4capital.com.
18. **Rainmatter Capital** (Zerodha's corporate VC arm): Bangalore-based, fintech and climate focused, with an average seed round size of about $1.5M and a strong India fintech network. A corporate-strategic angle, given Zerodha's own fintech distribution. Contact via rainmatter.com.

## Angel networks and individual angel channels

19. **Yatra Angel Network**: a fintech-focused early-stage angel network explicitly covering payments, lending, insurance, wealth, and inclusion startups, a plausible first stop for angel-stage capital given the multi-product (lending and insurance) shape of this venture. Contact via the network's public application or pitch-submission channel.
20. **LetsVenture and Indian Angel Network**: general India angel-investing platforms through which individual angels, including fintech- and impact-focused ones, can be reached via a structured pitch submission, rather than a single named investor. A useful aggregator channel for a solo founder without existing warm intros. Contact via letsventure.com or indianangelnetwork.com's pitch submission forms.
