# Setu (working name) — One-Pager

_Exploratory draft, week one of a cohort pilot (Hult Cohort Program, Phase 2 "Venture"). "Setu" (Hindi for "bridge") is a placeholder name, not finalized._

## Problem
Rural and informal-economy households in India earn near or below $2–3/day and have almost no buffer against small shocks — a sick cow, a slow work week, a medical bill. Without cheap, fast, small-scale credit or insurance, a shock costing a few dollars forces high-interest informal borrowing that spirals into debt (the "scarcity trap"). Formal digital credit in India is priced for salaried urban borrowers, not sub-$10 emergency needs; livestock-insurance penetration is only ~0.98% despite ~104.5 million rural households owning cattle (Swiss Re / PIB).

## Solution
A technology platform — not a direct lender — bundling four income-smoothing products around one household relationship, distributed through the SHG/MFI networks rural India already trusts:

1. **Emergency loans** sized for ~$1/day earners, sub-$10, reasonable interest
2. **Shock micro-insurance** for sick livestock and fluctuating work hours
3. **Fee-into-savings loans** — the fee funds a savings account in the borrower's name
4. **Harvest-to-stipend converter** — turns lump-sum harvest income into a predictable monthly stipend

No existing competitor bundles all four; the wedge is bundling plus trusted distribution, not cold-start credit scoring (the approach Tala/Branch use).

## Regulatory model
Real consumer lending in India requires RBI/NBFC registration or operation as a **Lending Service Provider (LSP)** for a licensed NBFC under RBI's Sep 2022 digital lending guidelines. Setu is structured as the tech/origination/servicing layer, partnering with a licensed NBFC (and a licensed insurer for the insurance line) — it does not hold a lending license itself and does not move real money in this pilot.

## Market
- **TAM:** India agri-fintech & rural credit market ≈ ₹1,200B (~$14.4B) (Ken Research)
- **SAM:** NABARD's SHG-Bank Linkage Programme — ~91 lakh SHGs, ~10 crore (100M) women, ~$25B FY2024 disbursement — the realistic distribution rail
- **SOM:** one pilot MFI/NGO/SHG-federation partnership, dozens to low hundreds of households
- Livestock-insurance coverage gap (~0.98% penetration of ~104.5M cattle-owning households) sizes the insurance line specifically

## Business model
Fee/commission-based, not balance-sheet lending: origination and servicing fees from the NBFC partner, insurance distribution commission, a platform fee on the savings-linked loan, and a conversion fee on the stipend product. Low-cost acquisition via existing SHG/MFI field relationships rather than paid digital acquisition.

## Traction (honest, week one)
No real users yet. What's real: product concept and four-line bundle defined; regulatory (NBFC/LSP) framing worked out; distribution channel identified and sized (NABARD SHG-BLP); competitive landscape mapped against 7 players. Customer discovery (5 target interviews) has not yet been conducted — flagged as the immediate next step, not retrofitted.

## Team
Solo founder/builder currently, building this as a Hult Cohort Program Phase 2 pilot. Near-term gaps: an India-based compliance/legal lead for the NBFC partnership, and a field-ops lead with SHG/MFI relationships.

## Financials
Illustrative only (full model + assumptions in `business-plan.md`): ~150 households and ~₹30,000 (~$360) revenue in Year 1, scaling to ~12,000 households and ~₹6,000,000 (~$72,000) by Year 3, likely at or near breakeven at best — all modeled planning assumptions, not results. Revenue is fee/commission-based; Setu does not carry credit risk or hold deposits itself.

## The ask
**Illustrative: $150,000–$250,000 pre-seed**, for NBFC/insurer partnership setup, MVP build, a first SHG-federation pilot, and customer discovery. Directional, not a firm modeled ask.
