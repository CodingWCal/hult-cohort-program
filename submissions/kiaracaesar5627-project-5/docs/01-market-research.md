## 1. Problem

People prepare for *an interview* as if it were one event. Hiring teams run *a job-specific conversation*: FP&A case math, CS behavioral + systems, consulting structure, CS manager de-escalation. Puzzle platforms and $200/hr coaches both miss that mapping.

Pain (observable, not surveyed here):

- Applicants grind the wrong prompt set, then stall on the first real interviewer question.
- Career centers cannot staff 1:1 mocks for every role.
- Employer-side tools (HireVue and peers) score candidates; they do not let the candidate rehearse the other side of the table.

## 2. Customer interviews

Curriculum asks for ≥5 structured interviews. **None are fabricated in this packet.**

### Protocol (used when a real conversation is booked)

- Who: job seeker currently in process, or a career-center operator, or a hiring manager.
- Length: 25–40 minutes.
- Questions: last interview they took; what they practiced; what still surprised them; what they would pay; what they would not trust an app to do.
- Recording: notes only; no names in the PR.

## 4. Competitors

| Player | What they are | Gap vs Interview Room |
|--------|----------------|------------------------|
| LeetCode / HackerRank | Puzzle + contest | Not an interviewer conversation for non-SWE roles |
| Big Interview | Video + coaching scripts | Broad; not 20 deep prompts per business role |
| Pramp / interviewing.io | Live peer / engineer mocks | Supply-constrained; SWE-weighted |
| HireVue / Modern Hire | Employer assessment | Opposite buyer |
| Career coaches | High-touch | Price and calendar, not a catalog |

Wedge: **interviewer-phrased questions mapped to the job application**, shipped for 25 roles (500 prompts) with playbooks.

## 5. Insight (desk only)

The category is crowded at “practice interviewing” and thin at “practice *this job’s* interview.” That is the product thesis. It remains untested by five primary interviews until those rows above are filled.
