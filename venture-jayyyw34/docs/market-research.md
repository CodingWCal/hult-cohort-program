# Market research: LocalPlate

## Problem

Home cooks and bakers already move extra food through private social channels. Neighbors who want that food cannot see a live, local inventory. National delivery marketplaces are built for restaurants that can pay courier economics. The gap is a same-day neighborhood board with remaining servings and pickup windows — not another 30-minute courier promise.

Evidence of the informal channel is public and repeated: WhatsApp “roti ready” statuses, Instagram Saturday bake stories, Facebook “who want pelau” posts in Trinidad and Tobago. Those are not LocalPlate interviews; they are the desk-research starting point.

## Market size

Figures are public-order-of-magnitude, not a commissioned TAM study.

- **TAM (desk):** People in Trinidad and Tobago paying someone else to cook — restaurants, street food, and homemade pots sold socially. LocalPlate is not chasing all restaurant GMV. Treat this as a ceiling, not a commissioned TAM study.
- **SAM (desk):** Informal homemade supply in Trinidad and Tobago plus buyers already hunting it on WhatsApp and Instagram. Food-handler and public-health rules vary; verify before onboarding a cook.
- **SOM (pilot assumption):** Towns listed in the product, including Couva, Penal, Point Fortin, Sangre Grande, Mayaro, and the original corridor / POS / San Fernando / Tobago set. Year-1 model in the business plan (~40 cooks) is an **assumption**, not a forecast from signed cooks.

Delivery-app take rates of roughly 15–30% are widely reported in restaurant-operator coverage of DoorDash and Uber Eats. That is the fee pressure LocalPlate is not trying to copy for six-plate menus.

## ICP

- **Cook:** Home baker or cook in Trinidad or Tobago who already sells or gifts food socially; can operate under applicable food-handler rules **or** a licensed kitchen; wants 4–20 plates to move the same day.
- **Buyer:** Neighbour, student, or office worker who will collect nearby; tired of delivery fees; wants doubles, roti, or Sunday lunch.
- **Not ICP (yet):** Full-service restaurants, nationwide shipping bakers, people who need ADA delivery, unpermitted hot-meal vendors we would be inducing to break local law.

## Wedge

Win **same-day, walkable inventory**. Incumbents win logistics and national SEO. LocalPlate wins the three-block radius where a cook can sell six plates without a car and a neighbor can eat food that still tastes like a house kitchen.
