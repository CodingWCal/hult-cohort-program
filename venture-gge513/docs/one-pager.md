# Latent — one-pager

**Say what you are trying to do. Meet the two or three people who could do it with you.**

Live: [latent-nu.vercel.app](https://latent-nu.vercel.app) · Repo: [gge513/latent](https://github.com/gge513/latent)

**What it is.** A proximity engine. One sentence in; two or three people whose *public work* sits closest, each with a written reason; a pick; a message to send. No visitor account. Nothing they type is stored. A person on the other side is reachable only after they claim their card.

**Why it exists.** The sharing economy made idle physical capacity reachable. It did not do the same for human capability people want to share or donate. Directories match on claims. Latent matches on what someone has actually done. The visitor keeps the judgment. The site does not claim taste. Speed of development is commodity; knowing which person is just ahead of you is not.

**What it is not.** A hiring portal, a leaderboard, a marketplace, or a private instance that sells a better view of other people. Each of those is a business one door over, and each is refused by commitment, not by taste.

**Now.** The first deployment is live on a 31-person summer builder cohort. Production counts as of 2026-08-16: **1 of 31 claimed · 17 picks · 14 messages taken to send.** Unique users are not reported: the events table has no name column, by design.

**Business.** This first instance takes $0, and matching stays free everywhere. Operators of bounded groups pay **$6,000 per instance, per season** — invoiced, setup included, no free pilots — for a front door: outsiders with one sentence reach the right two or three members. An instance passes or fails on the operator's 90-day verdict. Five operator conversations with zero closes by 2026-12-31 kills the model. One design decision stays open and disclosed: the exact evidence base future instances match on; if it moves away from bounded public work, the market claim moves with it.

**Ask.** Use the site. If it works, a conversation — we are not raising until the first paid instance passes its 90-day verdict. Founder: George Eastwood.
