## 1. Latent

Say one sentence about what you are trying to do.
Meet the two or three people whose work sits closest.

No account. No form. No ranking.

https://latent-nu.vercel.app

---

## 2. The problem

Capability exists. The infrastructure to reach it does not.

Directories match on claims.
Feeds match on recency.
Marketplaces match on inventory.

None of them find the person just ahead of you —
someone who has done the adjacent work, in public,
and can both do the job and bring you along.

---

## 3. The photograph

Development got faster: darkroom, one-hour lab, Polaroid, phone.

None of it made anyone a better photographer.

Speed is commodity. Judgment is not.

The visitor is the photographer. The site is the darkroom.

---

## 4. The product

1. You say or type one sentence.
2. Two or three people develop out of the dark, each with a written reason.
3. You pick one. You say why, if you want, in your own words.
4. The site hands you the message. You send it.

We store none of what you said.
A builder is reachable only if they have claimed their card.

---

## 5. Why this is not a hiring site

The obvious build was a talent directory with a request-intro button.

Independent reviewers of the early product said the same thing:
the match is the product, and a directory is a different one.

We kept the product.

Reachability is opted in. Unclaimed is latent. Latent cannot be mailed.

---

## 6. Traction (honest)

Live. Production. First deployment: 31 builders from one summer cohort.

As of 2026-08-16, from the public production endpoint (`/api/metrics`):

- **14** messages a visitor took away to send
- **17** picks — judgment exercised at the door
- **1 of 31** cards claimed (developed) — the honest adverse number

We do not report unique users. The events table has no name column,
so a per-person count cannot exist. That is a lock, not a gap in the dashboard.

## 7. The market we will name, and the one we will not

We will not claim LinkedIn’s billion members.

The only SAM this deck will speak: **operators of bounded groups**
(accelerators, fellowships, residencies) that already publish work.

One design decision is open, and we say so: the exact evidence base
future instances match on. If it moves away from bounded public work,
this slide is rewritten, not patched.

---

## 8. Business model

This instance: **$0**. The site takes nothing from visitors or builders, ever.

The product is a **front door**: operators of bounded groups pay
**$6,000 per instance, per season** — invoiced, setup included —
so an outsider with one sentence reaches the right two or three members.

Never for seeing a match. Never for being seen.
Never a private instance that sells asymmetric sight.
**No free pilots** — a $0 instance cannot fail the renewal question.

An instance passes or fails on the **operator's 90-day verdict**:
intros routed, members reached, would they renew.
Five operator conversations with zero closes by end of 2026 kills the model.
